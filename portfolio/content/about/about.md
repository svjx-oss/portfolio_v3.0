## A little about me

The work I enjoy most starts with understanding the real constraints: what needs to be dependable, where a system can fail, and how people will make sense of it when the implementation is no longer visible. From there, the goal is to make the essential path straightforward without hiding the decisions that matter.

I enjoy turning complex technical work into clear, dependable experiences.

I build software across embedded systems, developer tools, and product infrastructure. I care about the details that make technology feel reliable and understandable for the people who use it.
